## Electron 2026-09-11 Changelog (v0.0.3)

### Content and Balances
- Quantum Link nerfed: Enemy loses HP equal to the stack amount per trigger (does not matter how much Farad or HP was lost).
- Changes due to Quantum Link nerf:
  - Plug In: Lose 2 ⇒ 1 Farad.
  - Superposition: At the start of turn, apply 1(2) ⇒ 2(3) Quantum Link.
  - Destabilize: Apply 2(3) ⇒ 3(4) Quantum Link.

- Flux Capacitor buffed: Gain 2X(+2) ⇒ 3X(+2) Farad.
- EM Pulse nerfed/adjusted: Gain 6(7) ⇒ 6(9) Block. Apply 1(2) ⇒ 1(1) Weak.
- Vampiric Draw nerfed: Increase Farad gain by 1(2) ⇒ 1(1).
- Aleph Null nerfed: Prevent the next 2(3) times you would lose HP from Drain instead of preventing it for a turn. (should prevent infinites)
- Alloy Armor nerfed: Gain 10(13) ⇒ 8(11) Block.
- Demon Core adjusted: Gain 7(10) Spin. Empty: [Gain 2(3) Stability.]
- Power Bank adjusted: Cost 2 ⇒ 2(1). No longer gain Drain on upgrade.
- Tail Whip adjusted: Deal 7(8) ⇒ 7(9) damage. Deplete: [Hit 1(2) additional time => Apply 2(3) Weak.]
- Neutron Collector adjusted: Cost 2(1) ⇒ 2. Gain Drain on upgrade.
- Scattering reworked: Apply 6 Quantum Link to ALL enemies. Empty: [Increase Quantum Link by 3(5) and return this card to your Hand next turn]

- Into the Void removed.
- Added new card: Positron Beam - 1 Cost Uncommon Attack - Deal 8(9) Damage and Apply 1(2) Quantum Link to ALL enemies. Empty: [Gain 2(3) Farad then Lose 1 Farad.]
- Added new card: Observer Pattern - 1 Cost Uncommon Power - Whenever you apply Quantum Link, gain Block equal to the amount applied.
- Added new card: Wave Dash - 0 Cost Rare Attack - Spend 3 Farad to deal 12(15) damage to ALL enemies and gain 12(15) Block.

- Fission Cell moved to Rare.

### Misc
