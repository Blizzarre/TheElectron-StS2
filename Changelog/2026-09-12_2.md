## Electron 2026-09-12_2 Changelog (v0.0.6)

### Content and Balances
- Positron Beam nerfed: Deal 8(10) ⇒ 6(8) damage to ALL enemies. (to not powercreep Binding Whip)
- Observer Pattern nerfed: 1 ⇒ 2(1) cost. No longer upgrades to Innate.
- String Theory buffed: 2 ⇒ 1 cost. 
- Entwine adjusted: Apply 4(6) Quantum Link. Gain 1 Farad. (No longer deplete)
- Entangle adjusted: Apply 10(14) Quantum Link. Gain 3 Farad. (No longer deplete)
- Vortex adjusted: Attack ⇒ Skill. Gain 2 Spin for each unique Quark (+2).

### Misc
- Memory Leak tweaked: Gain Farad then Draw card.
- Universal Flavor tweaked: Produce Top before Bottom Quark.
- Fusion will now gain Energy before drawing cards.

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed Emergency Protocol crashing the game.
- Fixed String Theory applying Quantum Link to yourself when you do self damage. (causing Quantum Link to do infinite damage to yourself.)
