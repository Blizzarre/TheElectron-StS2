## Electron 2026-09-13 Changelog (v0.0.8)

### Content and Balances
- Fusion will now deal damage (and self damage) in multiple hits.
- Modify buffed: Gain 2(3) ⇒ 3(4) Strength.
- Cathode Ray buffed: +Drain. Deal 14(16) ⇒ 14(17) damage. Deplete: [Deal 5(7) ⇒ 6(9) additional damage.]
- Aleph Null nerfed: Draw 2(3) ⇒ 2(2) cards.
- Lightspeed nerfed: Add Drain to ALL cards in your Hand **this turn**.
- Double Slit nerfed/adjusted: Deal 4(5) ⇒ 4(6) damage. Deal 1(2) ⇒ 1(1) additional damage for each Quark.
  - Given that the max additional damage you can have at the start is +2, it can deserve to be a bit better than twin strike (especially when the character doesn't gain Str like Ironclad)
- Spaghettify adjusted: Deal 10 damage 5(6) times.
- Reroute moved to Uncommon and buffed: Gain 4(5) ⇒ 5(6) Farad.
- Ex Nihilo adjusted: Cost 1 ⇒ 0. Deal 0 damage. Deal 5(7) additional for each Empty card you played this combat.
- Accumulator reworked: Deal 9(12) damage. Deplete: [Increase the number of hits by 1 this combat.]
- Condense buffed: Empty: [Gain 1(1) ⇒ 1(2) Energy next turn.]
- Perpetual Motion reworked: X Cost Rare Power - Increase the effectiveness of your Up and Down Quarks by X(+1).
- Overvolt moved from Rare to Uncommon and adjusted: Deal 6(9) ⇒ 3(3) damage to ALL enemies X(+1) times. Lose X ⇒ 2 Farad.
- Wave Dash moved from Rare to Uncommon and adjusted: Spend 3 ⇒ 2 Farad to Deal 12(15) => 9(11) damage to ALL enemies and gain 12(15) => 9(11) Block.
- Endless Assault moved from Uncommon to Rare and adjusted: Drain. Deal 10(13) => 12(15) damage. If you lost Farad, return this card to your Hand.
- Added a new card: Anion - 1 Cost Common Skill - Gain 3(4) Farad. Empty: [Gain 1(2) Farad.]
- Removed Flux Capacitor.

### Misc
- Renamed 'Disposable Battery' to 'Hydrogen Fuel'
- Adjusted the indicator on cards so that the Drain icon is always on the leftmost side.

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed Fusion not working properly if no enemies can be targeted.
